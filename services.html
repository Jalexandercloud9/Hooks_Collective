Services

The Hooks Collective partners with communities, schools, organizations, and professionals to create thoughtful, engaging experiences centered on mental health and connection.

Youth Programming

Interactive programs that give young people a safe space to talk openly, build practical skills, and explore the challenges affecting their everyday lives.

Community Workshops

Engaging workshops and guided conversations that bring mental health and emotional wellness education into schools, churches, and community organizations.

Professional Training

Practical training for educators, school teams, youth serving professionals, and organizations that want to better understand, engage, and support the people they serve.

Consulting

I work with schools, organizations, and community partners to develop, strengthen, or rethink programs and initiatives with the people they serve at the center.

Mental Health Advocacy

Through speaking, partnerships, and community initiatives, I help move mental health conversations forward, increase understanding, challenge stigma, and bring greater attention to communities where access and representation still matter.
