# Assets

Drop client photos here with these exact filenames — the site picks them up automatically.
Until they exist, the page falls back to gradient placeholders.

- `hero.jpg` — landscape, min 2000px wide. Allana speaking, or an environmental portrait.
  Keep the subject right-of-center; the left side is covered by the headline overlay.
- `allana.jpg` — portrait orientation, 4:5 crop, min 1200px wide.
- `og-image.jpg` — 1200x630, used for link previews in texts, Slack, and social.

Compress everything before committing (squoosh.app, target under 400KB each).
